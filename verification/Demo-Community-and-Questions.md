## Behavior and architecture

- The user's explicit request for fictional social examples supersedes the previous empty-comments/peers preference. Each real video has three original Arabic comments about its topic and four fictional leaderboard peers. Sample badges, discussion disclosure and leaderboard labels distinguish these from actual learners and YouTube comments. The original silhouette is reused; no new external asset was downloaded.
- Typed fixtures live in `src/data/demo-community.ts`. Comment slots remain server-rendered through `CommentItem`; new posts remain client-owned, newest-first and independently persisted. Samples never enter the localStorage record or award personal completion. Initial personal progress remains zero; leaderboard ties and the highlighted personal row use actual saved lesson counts.
- Reusable `TypingMotivation` shows original level-dependent Arabic messages, types Unicode code points at 30ms intervals and rotates every 4.5 seconds. Pausing shows the complete current message. Reduced motion disables typing and rotation. Character updates are hidden from assistive technology; a complete accessible message is available without repeated live announcements. Both timers are cleared on pause, preference change or unmount.
- The shared text-entry form exposes a post-submit slot. The question dialog displays the exact requested thank-you sentence and a separate local-saving/simulated-review notice. Ask another question clears the confirmation and focuses the same empty textarea without duplicating a post. Explore more videos closes the dialog, releases the body lock and reaches the catalogue anchor. No instructor/moderation delivery or backend is connected.
- README and asset provenance were updated. No dependency, storage schema, content-version or deployment configuration change was needed.
