## Behavior and architecture

The catalogue combines search, topic and learning-status filters; sorts by featured order, name or progress; announces result counts; and offers clear/empty recovery that returns focus to search. The resume card uses the first course in progress and its remembered lesson, with an unstarted-course fallback. Counts and statuses use the existing provider-owned learning store. Baseline history remains simulated; subsequent completion is derived from actual local actions. Filters reset on reload. No backend, store schema or dependency change was introduced.

Server routes serialize course summaries and relevant reference records. `CourseCatalogue` owns page filters; existing `CourseCard` renders course state. `ReferenceCollection` shares catalogue cards and a compact detail list; its native `ReferenceDialog` loads dynamically. Detail references sit below the existing comment form, outside the bounded comment history. The core player/materials/curriculum/PDF/exam placement is retained. The catalogue’s styling freedom comes from REQ-004; exact core Figma assets and physical acceptance remain open.

The three catalogue collections are Webology, computer science and Arabic C++. Related detail references add general learning skills. All four video IDs and three playlist IDs were observed on the requested creator’s channel/watch pages. [Dated source provenance](catalogue-20261010-reference-sources.json) records IDs, titles, ownership evidence and access limits; [ASSETS.md](../ASSETS.md) records attribution and rights limits. Descriptions and associations are editorial summaries. Ali Shahin did not author or endorse our fictional practice syllabi, learners, PDFs or exams.

No YouTube request or downloaded creator thumbnail precedes **Load YouTube player**. After that explicit action, a privacy-enhanced iframe offers native playback and source/collection/channel links. It has inline/fullscreen permissions, a nonempty origin referrer and no autoplay. `rel=0` restricts related videos to the same channel; it does not disable them. Captions and availability remain under YouTube’s control. Closing removes the iframe; reopening requires loading again. Viewing a reference never credits practice completion. Official technical sources checked on 10 October: [player parameters](https://developers.google.com/youtube/player_parameters), [embed and privacy-enhanced behavior](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780?hl=en).
